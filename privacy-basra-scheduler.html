Privacy Policy — Basra Scheduler

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Operated by Basra Developers

Basra Scheduler is an internal tool operated by Basra Developers. It is used by our own team only, to schedule and publish our own content to Facebook Pages and an Instagram account that our business owns or manages, and to read basic engagement information for those same assets. It is not offered to the public, and no outside user can sign up for, log in to, or use this app.

Information the app accesses

Through Meta's official APIs, the app accesses only information about our own business assets: Page names and IDs, the content we publish, scheduled-post information, and aggregate engagement statistics (such as follower counts). The app does not collect, access, or store personal data about Facebook or Instagram users, and it does not read private messages or personal profiles.

How information is used

Information is used solely to operate our own publishing workflow: creating, scheduling, verifying, and reporting on posts to our own Pages and Instagram account. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer any information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data storage and retention

The app stores only the access credentials required to operate and minimal operational logs. No end-user personal data is stored. Operational records are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Data deletion requests

Because the app does not collect personal data about platform users, there is normally nothing to delete. If you believe the app holds any information relating to you, contact us at the address below and we will review and delete it promptly.
